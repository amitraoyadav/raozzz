Frequently Asked Questions

Whether you have just one or a dozen questions for us, you've come to the right place!

We know navigating through the planning process of a destination celebration can be a bit tricky. That's why we're here! To answer all of your frequently asked destination wedding questions (and any you may have about our services as well!)

Why do I Need a Wedding Planner?

A wedding planner is essential for a smooth and stress-free wedding. They bring expertise, experience, and vendor connections to ensure a well-organized event. They save you time, handle logistics, and help you stay within budget. With their guidance, you can make informed decisions and have peace of mind, allowing you to fully enjoy your special day without the stress of managing every detail.

How much does your wedding planning service cost?

The cost of our wedding planning services can vary depending on several factors, including the complexity of the event, the number of guests, and the specific services you require. We offer customized packages to suit different budgets and preferences. To provide you with an accurate cost estimate, we would need to assess your specific needs and vision for your wedding. We encourage you to contact us directly so we can discuss your requirements and provide you with a personalized quote tailored to your unique wedding planning needs. We strive to offer competitive pricing while delivering exceptional service and creating unforgettable wedding experiences.

What is included in wedding planning services?

Wedding planning services include: Decor and Design, Destination and Venue Entertainment, Pre Wedding celebrations, Food and Beverages, wedding budget, return gifts, and many others. By choosing SMLW to arrange your wedding from beginning to realisation, we do so in the most practical manner while ensuring glamour.

What are the benefits to do a destination wedding?

Destination weddings offer several benefits, including a unique and memorable experience for you and your guests. They allow you to combine your wedding and honeymoon in one location, potentially saving on travel costs. Destination weddings also provide an intimate setting and quality time with loved ones. Plus, they offer picturesque backdrops and the opportunity to explore new destinations, creating unforgettable memories for everyone involved.

Do you offer customizable wedding packages?

Yes, we offer customizable wedding packages to cater to the unique needs and preferences of each couple. We understand that every wedding is different, and our goal is to provide a personalized experience that aligns with your vision. Our wedding planners will work closely with you to understand your requirements and design a package that includes the specific services you desire. Whether you need full-service planning or assistance with specific aspects of your wedding, we are dedicated to creating a package that suits your individual needs and ensures a memorable and stress-free wedding day.

Can you assist with managing our budget?

Absolutely! Managing your wedding budget is an essential part of our wedding planning services. We understand the importance of maximizing your resources while creating the wedding of your dreams. Our experienced wedding planners will work closely with you to establish a realistic budget, identify areas where costs can be optimized, and provide guidance on allocating funds effectively. We will help you make informed decisions, provide cost-saving tips, and negotiate with vendors to ensure you get the most value for your investment while staying within your budgetary constraints.

How many people from your team will be present?

The number of team members from our company present at a wedding event varies depending on the size, complexity, and specific requirements of the event. We typically assess the needs of each wedding and allocate a team accordingly. For smaller, more intimate weddings, four or five team members may be sufficient to oversee the event. However, for larger weddings or those with intricate logistics, we may have a team of twenty or more professionals present. Our goal is to ensure that we have an adequate number of skilled professionals on-site to manage all aspects of the event and provide the highest level of service and coordination for our clients.

How do you handle rainy or inclement weather on the wedding day?

In the event of rainy or inclement weather on the wedding day, we have contingency plans in place. We work closely with the venue to identify indoor or covered spaces as backup options. If necessary, we can arrange for tent rentals to create a sheltered area. We maintain communication with vendors to ensure they are prepared for weather-related adjustments. Your comfort and enjoyment are our priority, rain or shine.

What happens if there are changes or emergencies on the wedding day?

In the event of changes or emergencies on the wedding day, our team is well-prepared to handle them swiftly and effectively. We have contingency plans in place to address unforeseen circumstances. Our wedding planners will assess the situation, communicate with relevant parties, and work quickly to find suitable solutions while minimizing any disruption to the event. We maintain strong relationships with vendors and have a network of resources to rely on in case of emergencies. Our priority is to ensure that the couple's vision is realized, and we will go above and beyond to adapt and resolve any challenges that arise, ensuring a seamless and memorable wedding day.
